Scientific contract

How to read the table

The visualization is a view over the canonical dataset—not a second source of scientific truth.

1 · Identity

Ask what is oscillating? Equal numerical frequency does not make two phenomena physically identical.

2 · Frequency

Ranges, characteristic lines, detector bands and biological overlays keep their original meanings. Hertz does not erase context.

3 · Interaction

Coupling needs a mechanism, mediator and conservation conditions. Same-frequency overlap alone is not a bridge.

4 · Energy

Every energy claim needs a system boundary. Energy may store, transfer, convert, radiate, dissipate or disperse; ordinary processes do not create it from nothing.

5 · Evidence

Established, model-dependent, open, speculative and contested records remain visually and semantically distinct.

6 · Unknowns

Unknown carrier, unknown frequency and missing instrumentation are useful data states. They are not invitations to fabricate a bar.
