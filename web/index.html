Scientific contract

How to read the universe without fooling ourselves.

Identity

Ask what is oscillating? Equal numerical frequency does not make two phenomena physically identical.

02

Frequency

Ranges, characteristic lines, detector bands, and biological overlays keep their original meanings. Hertz does not erase context.

03

Interaction

Coupling needs a mechanism, mediator, and conservation conditions. Same-frequency overlap alone is not a bridge.

04

Energy

Every energy claim needs a system boundary. Ordinary processes do not create energy from nothing.

05

Evidence

Established, model-dependent, open, speculative, and contested records remain visibly distinct.

06

Unknowns

Unknown carrier, unknown frequency, and missing instrumentation are useful data states—not invitations to fabricate a bar.
