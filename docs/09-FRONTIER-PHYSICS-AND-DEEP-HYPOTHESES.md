# Phase 4 — Frontier Physics, Consciousness & Deep Hypotheses

**Status:** Phase 4 research layer  
**Purpose:** Add unresolved physics and serious human questions without erasing the boundary between observation, hypothesis, philosophy, and established physical law.

---

# 1. Why Phase 4 needs two frontier layers

The project now separates frontier material into two related but distinct layers.

## A. Physical frontier

Questions already expressible in physics through fields, particles, interactions, observables, detector response, or cosmological parameters.

Examples:

- dark matter;
- axions and axion-like particles;
- dark photons;
- ultralight fields;
- fifth forces;
- dark energy;
- vacuum / zero-point phenomena;
- poorly covered gravitational-wave bands;
- quantum gravity.

## B. Human-experience frontier

Questions that are scientifically important but do not yet have an agreed physical ontology.

Examples:

- how memory is stored and retrieved;
- what consciousness is;
- whether consciousness requires only known neural mechanisms;
- whether electromagnetic or quantum effects play a special role in consciousness;
- terminal lucidity;
- near-death experiences;
- claims of anomalous information reception;
- the soul;
- survival of consciousness after irreversible death.

The second layer is **not excluded** from the project.

It is instead protected from premature classification.

A question can be profound before it is a physics variable.

---

# 2. Frontier admission rule

Every frontier entry must answer as many of these as possible:

```
question
→ hypothesis/model
→ what physically exists in that model?
→ what would oscillate or vary?
→ what would it couple to?
→ what observable would differ from established physics?
→ what experiment could falsify it?
→ what has actually been measured?
→ what remains unexplained?
```

If the first physical step cannot yet be defined, the entry remains a **QUESTION NODE**, not a frequency bar.

---

# 3. Evidence labels expanded for Phase 4

Phase 4 retains the existing labels and adds two presentation flags.

## ESTABLISHED

Observed or reproducibly measured within a mature framework.

## MODEL-DEPENDENT

A legitimate quantitative prediction conditional on a specified theory or candidate model.

## OPEN

A real unresolved scientific question.

## SPECULATIVE

A proposed explanatory idea without sufficient empirical support to present as established.

## CONTESTED EVIDENCE

A literature reports statistically or phenomenologically interesting results, but interpretation, methodology, replication, or causal explanation remains disputed.

## NOT YET OPERATIONALIZED

The concept lacks a sufficiently precise measurable physical definition for placement as a field, particle, force, or frequency.

These flags are not insults. They tell us what kind of knowledge we actually have.

# PART I — FRONTIER PHYSICS

# 4. Dark matter

The gravitational and cosmological evidence for an unseen matter component is strong, but its microscopic identity remains unknown.

Dark matter should therefore be represented as:

```
observed gravitational/cosmological effects
        ↓
unknown microscopic identity
        ↓
candidate models
        ↓
candidate-specific couplings and spectra
        ↓
experiments / exclusions / open parameter space
```

There is no single "dark-matter frequency."

Different candidates imply very different observables.

---

# 5. Axions and axion-like particles

Ultralight axion/ALP dark matter can behave approximately as a coherent oscillating classical field in appropriate mass ranges.

For a nonrelativistic bosonic field, the leading oscillation scale is tied to rest energy:

```
f ≈ m c² / h
```

with coherence corrections from the dark-matter velocity distribution.

Possible couplings include:

- nuclear and electron spin precession;
- electromagnetic conversion in magnetic fields or plasmas;
- atomic-clock shifts in some scalar/ALP models;
- transient signatures from topological defects in specified models.

Current experiments use resonant cavities, spin systems, precision clocks, interferometers, and distributed quantum sensors to search pieces of this parameter space.

### 2026 example

A Nature experiment published in January 2026 used a distributed network of noble-gas spin sensors to constrain axion topological-defect dark matter over a specified model-dependent mass region.

This is exactly how Phase 4 should represent unknown physics:

> not "axions are there," but **model → coupling → predicted signal → searched region → constraint**.

---

# 6. Dark photons

Dark photons are hypothetical additional vector bosons.

Some models permit kinetic mixing with the ordinary photon. If ultralight dark photons constitute dark matter, the field can behave coherently and can sometimes convert to ordinary electromagnetic radiation in an appropriate plasma environment.

A 2024 LOFAR analysis searched for monochromatic radio signals from resonant conversion of ultralight dark-photon or axion dark matter in the solar corona over **30–80 MHz**.

### Classification

**MODEL-DEPENDENT / SEARCHED**

The radio band belongs to the predicted converted electromagnetic signal in that specified scenario. It is not a universal "dark photon frequency band."

---

# 7. Topological-defect dark matter

Some field theories permit macroscopic structures such as:

- domain walls;
- strings;
- localized field configurations.

If Earth or a sensor network passes through such a structure, the signature could be a **transient correlated event** rather than a persistent sinusoidal frequency.

This matters because the future atlas must handle:

- continuous oscillations;
- spectral lines;
- broadband signals;
- and **transients**.

Not every frontier phenomenon belongs on a horizontal frequency bar.

---

# 8. Fifth forces and new interactions

The Standard Model plus general relativity does not exclude every possible weak new interaction.

Precision spectroscopy can search for hypothetical bosons that would mediate an additional force.

Modern isotope-shift spectroscopy, for example, can test for departures from Standard-Model expectations that could indicate a new boson coupling to electrons and neutrons.

### Classification

**MODEL-DEPENDENT / OPEN SEARCH**

A fifth force is not an established new force.

The meaningful atlas object is:

```
mediator mass
+ coupling strengths
+ interaction range
+ observable deviation
+ exclusion region
```

---

# 9. Dark energy

Dark energy is inferred from the accelerated expansion of the Universe, but its physical origin remains unknown.

Candidate descriptions include:

- a cosmological constant;
- dynamical scalar-field models such as quintessence;
- interacting dark-sector models;
- modified-gravity explanations.

### Current status as of September 2026

DESI's first-three-year results strengthened earlier hints that a time-varying dark-energy model could fit combined datasets better than a constant cosmological constant in some analyses.

However, a **July 30, 2026 DESI DR2 Lyman-alpha full-shape analysis shifted toward the standard ΛCDM prediction** and DESI explicitly noted that the evolving-dark-energy hints might fade, or that a more complicated model may be required.

Therefore:

**Evolving dark energy is not established.**

The correct project label is:

**OPEN — CURRENT OBSERVATIONAL TENSION / MODEL COMPARISON**

Dark energy also does not presently have a meaningful universal "frequency."

---

# 10. Vacuum fluctuations and zero-point energy

Quantum systems retain ground-state fluctuations.

Vacuum/zero-point phenomena are real parts of quantum theory and have measurable consequences, including effects related to quantum fluctuations and Casimir physics.

This is where the phrase **"energy all around us"** needs scientific precision.

There are physically measurable electromagnetic and quantum fields everywhere, and matter is immersed in thermal radiation, background electromagnetic fields, gravitational fields, and quantum vacuum structure.

But:

> **the existence of vacuum fluctuations does not establish an unlimited extractable free-energy source.**

NIST explicitly distinguishes measurable zero-point fluctuations from a reservoir from which real work can simply be extracted.

### Project classification

- zero-point fluctuations: **ESTABLISHED**
- Casimir-type vacuum effects: **ESTABLISHED**
- unlimited usable vacuum/free-energy extraction: **NOT ESTABLISHED**
- particular proposed energy-extraction cycles: evaluate case by case against thermodynamics and experiment.

---

# 11. Gravitational-wave coverage gaps

Phase 2 recorded observational bands.

Phase 4 asks what lies between them.

A particularly interesting proposed observational gap is approximately:

```
0.1 Hz – 1 Hz
```

between the upper end of LISA-like space sensitivity and the lower ambitions of next-generation terrestrial detectors.

Lunar and other detector concepts are being discussed partly because this region may contain astrophysical and early-Universe information.

At much higher frequencies, MHz–GHz gravitational-wave searches remain experimentally challenging and largely unexplored compared with the directly detected ground-based band.

### Classification

**INSTRUMENT / COVERAGE FRONTIER**

A detector gap is not evidence for unknown physics by itself.

---

# 12. Quantum gravity

Whether gravity is fundamentally quantum remains unresolved experimentally.

Proposed experiments seek signatures such as gravity-mediated quantum correlations between mesoscopic masses.

However, theoretical work published in 2025 showed that some classical-gravity constructions can also produce entanglement-like outcomes that had previously been proposed as decisive quantum-gravity evidence.

Therefore even the definition of a "smoking gun" test remains under active debate.

### Classification

**OPEN FUNDAMENTAL PHYSICS**

Do not assign a "quantum-gravity frequency band" unless a specific model predicts an observable spectrum.

# PART I-B — UNKNOWN / UNOBSERVED PROPOSED ENERGY & INFORMATION CARRIERS

This category is deliberately broad enough to hold ideas that have **not yet earned a known physics row**.

The visible label may be:

> **Unknown / Unobserved Energy & Information Forms**

But the technical meaning is more careful.

In modern physics, **energy is a property of a physical system, not usually a substance or fluid by itself**. Therefore an unexplained phenomenon should not automatically be called "unknown energy."

A future discovery might instead be:

- a previously unknown field;
- a new particle;
- a new interaction or force;
- a new collective excitation;
- a hidden-sector degree of freedom;
- an information-bearing physical substrate;
- an unknown coupling between known systems;
- a transient rather than periodic phenomenon;
- or a genuinely new energy-transfer mechanism.

The frontier category therefore asks:

```
UNKNOWN EFFECT?
    ↓
UNKNOWN CARRIER?
    ├─ field?
    ├─ particle?
    ├─ force / interaction?
    ├─ information channel?
    ├─ collective state?
    ├─ transient?
    └─ no physical mechanism yet identified
```

## Admission rule

An item can enter this category even before its carrier is known, but it must keep separate fields for:

- **reported phenomenon**;
- **evidence status**;
- **known conventional explanations tested**;
- **hypothesized carrier**;
- **whether energy transfer is actually demonstrated**;
- **whether information transfer is claimed**;
- **distance dependence**;
- **shielding dependence**;
- **time dependence / frequency**, if any;
- **reproducibility**;
- **falsification test**.

This category is a **holding region for unknowns**, not a declaration that unknown energy has been detected.

---

# 12A. Black holes

Black holes are **established astrophysical objects**.

Evidence includes:

- stellar and gas dynamics around compact massive objects;
- gravitational-wave detections from black-hole mergers;
- event-horizon-scale imaging of M87* and Sagittarius A*;
- ringdown signals consistent with the behavior predicted for Kerr black holes.

The Event Horizon Telescope's observations of Sagittarius A* provide direct evidence for a supermassive black hole at the center of the Milky Way, with the observed ring size consistent with Kerr-metric expectations.

### Frequency treatment

A black hole does **not** have one universal frequency.

Perturbed black holes have **quasinormal modes**. Their ringdown frequencies and damping times depend primarily on:

- mass;
- spin;
- mode index.

For a given remnant black hole:

```
mass + spin
    → allowed quasinormal-mode spectrum
    → gravitational-wave ringdown
```

LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA observations now use black-hole spectroscopy to compare measured ringdown tones with general-relativity predictions.

Black-hole systems can also show:

- orbital frequencies;
- accretion-disk variability;
- quasi-periodic oscillations;
- jet variability;
- merger/inspiral gravitational-wave frequencies.

These belong to the surrounding system or dynamical state, not one intrinsic "black-hole frequency."

### Classification

**BLACK HOLES: ESTABLISHED**

**QUASINORMAL-MODE SPECTRUM: ESTABLISHED GENERAL-RELATIVITY PREDICTION WITH OBSERVATIONAL TESTS**

---

# 12B. The black-hole singularity

Classical general relativity predicts singular behavior inside idealized black-hole solutions.

In the simplest description, curvature and density become formally divergent at a singularity.

However, this is **not directly observed**.

NASA explicitly notes that the singularity may represent either a physical feature or a sign that classical general relativity has reached the limit of its applicability and must be replaced by a quantum theory of gravity.

The atlas therefore must not draw the singularity as though it were an experimentally imaged point at the center of every black hole.

A safer representation is:

```
black hole / event horizon: observed astrophysical object
        ↓
classical GR interior evolution
        ↓
singularity prediction
        ↓
unknown quantum-gravity completion
```

### Frequency treatment

There is no established "singularity frequency."

A singularity is not presently a known oscillatory degree of freedom.

Specific quantum-gravity models may eventually replace the classical singularity with:

- a bounce;
- a quantum core;
- a regularized high-curvature region;
- new degrees of freedom;
- some other structure.

Those would require model-specific observables.

### Classification

**CLASSICAL SINGULARITY: THEORETICAL PREDICTION / NOT DIRECTLY OBSERVED**

**PHYSICAL NATURE OF THE CENTRAL REGION: OPEN**

---

# 12C. Wormholes

Wormholes are mathematically allowed spacetime geometries in some solutions or extensions of gravitational theory.

They are **not observationally established astrophysical objects**.

A traversable wormhole would, in broad terms, connect otherwise distant regions of spacetime through a throat.

Theoretical difficulty is substantial.

Many familiar traversable-wormhole constructions require:

- exotic stress-energy;
- violations of classical energy conditions;
- modified gravity;
- or special quantum effects.

Recent theoretical work continues to explore whether particular configurations can exist consistently, while other analyses derive strong no-go or inaccessibility results under specified assumptions.

### Frequency treatment

There is no universal "wormhole frequency."

A specific wormhole model could in principle predict observables such as:

- gravitational-wave echoes or altered ringdown;
- lensing signatures;
- orbital anomalies;
- electromagnetic/accretion signatures;
- resonant modes of the throat;
- characteristic instability timescales.

Those predictions are model-dependent.

### Atlas treatment

```
wormhole geometry
    → model assumptions
    → predicted observable
    → detector / astronomical search
    → constraint
```

### Classification

**WORMHOLES: THEORETICAL / UNOBSERVED**

**TRAVERSABLE WORMHOLES: HIGHLY MODEL-DEPENDENT**

# PART II — MEMORY & CONSCIOUSNESS

# 13. How do we "communicate with" memory?

The scientifically grounded version of this question is:

> **How does a present brain state retrieve, reconstruct, and link information encoded by past brain states?**

Current neuroscience provides a partial answer through **memory engrams**.

Engrams are neuronal ensembles associated with memory encoding and retrieval.

Evidence includes:

- reactivation of neurons involved in encoding during later recall;
- disruption of selected engram cells impairing recall in experimental models;
- experimental activation of tagged engram cells inducing or recovering memory-related behavior in animal studies;
- overlap and reactivation mechanisms that can link distinct memories.

This is remarkable, but it does not imply that a memory is stored at one frequency.

Memory retrieval involves:

- cellular ensembles;
- synaptic changes;
- hippocampal/cortical circuits;
- replay/reactivation;
- oscillatory coordination across multiple timescales.

### Classification

**ENGRAM-BASED MEMORY RETRIEVAL: ESTABLISHED RESEARCH FRAMEWORK**

The full physical code of human memory remains incomplete.

---

# 14. Neural rhythms are real; a "memory frequency" is not established

Brain activity contains measurable rhythms.

Phase synchronization, phase resetting, cross-frequency coupling, and other oscillatory dynamics are actively studied in perception, memory, and multisensory integration.

But the project must reject claims such as:

> "memory lives at 7.83 Hz"

or:

> "consciousness is 40 Hz"

unless a specific experiment and operational meaning are supplied.

Neural rhythms are **network dynamics**, not evidence of one master frequency of mind.

---

# 15. What is consciousness?

There is no single accepted scientific theory explaining why or how subjective experience arises.

Major active approaches include:

- Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT);
- Integrated Information Theory (IIT);
- higher-order theories;
- recurrent/re-entry theories;
- predictive-processing approaches.

A large adversarial collaboration published in Nature in 2025 tested predictions of GNWT and IIT directly rather than assuming either theory was correct.

### Classification

**CONSCIOUS EXPERIENCE EXISTS: ESTABLISHED**

**MECHANISM / BEST THEORY: OPEN**

This distinction matters.

We do not need to doubt consciousness to admit that its physical explanation is unfinished.

---

# 16. Electromagnetic-field theories of consciousness

Some researchers propose that endogenous electromagnetic fields generated by neural activity do more than correlate with neural processing — that the field itself may contribute essentially to conscious integration or qualia.

These proposals are scientifically discussable because electromagnetic fields are real and measurable.

But the stronger claim:

> "the brain's EM field is consciousness"

is **not established**.

Current literature treats multiple electromagnetic-field consciousness theories as proposals with unresolved empirical challenges.

### Atlas treatment

```
known neural electrical activity
→ known endogenous EM fields
→ proposed special consciousness role
→ testable predictions required
```

Classification:

**MODEL-DEPENDENT / SPECULATIVE**

---

# 17. Quantum consciousness and Orch OR

The Penrose–Hameroff Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch OR) family proposes that quantum processes associated with neuronal microtubules participate fundamentally in consciousness, with objective reduction linked to spacetime/quantum-gravity ideas.

The proposal continues to generate publications, including supportive work.

It has also faced substantial biological and physical criticism.

A 2026 review of quantum and non-classical consciousness approaches concluded that, under operational standards used in controlled quantum systems, **no study had yet demonstrated the required neural entanglement, long-lived coherence, or collapse dynamics**, and replication remains limited.

Therefore Phase 4 includes Orch OR because it is a concrete deep hypothesis with possible physical predictions — but labels it correctly.

### Classification

**SPECULATIVE / ACTIVE HYPOTHESIS**

Not established neuroscience.

Not established quantum gravity.

Not evidence for an afterlife.

---

# 18. The "soul" question

"Soul" has many religious, philosophical, and cultural meanings.

Physics cannot test a concept until the proposed physical consequences are specified.

A hypothetical physically interacting soul would need some operational description such as:

- what state variables describe it?
- does it carry energy or momentum?
- does it carry information?
- does it couple to matter or known fields?
- can it change neuronal states?
- does it persist when brain function irreversibly ceases?
- what measurement would distinguish it from neural processes?
- what conservation laws apply?

No established physical field, particle, force, carrier, or frequency has been identified as "the soul."

### Classification

**PHILOSOPHICAL / THEOLOGICAL CONCEPT — NOT YET OPERATIONALIZED AS PHYSICS**

This does not tell a person what to believe.

It tells the atlas what it can currently measure.

---

# 19. Near-death experiences

Near-death experiences (NDEs) and recalled experiences surrounding resuscitation are genuine reported human experiences and a legitimate research topic.

In AWARE-II, a prospective multicenter cardiac-arrest study:

- 567 in-hospital cardiac arrests were studied;
- 53 survived;
- 28 survivors completed interviews;
- 11 of those interviewed reported memories/perceptions suggestive of consciousness under the study's categories;
- no participant identified the hidden visual target;
- 1 of 28 identified the auditory stimulus;
- EEG patterns described as compatible with network-level cognitive activity appeared during some resuscitations.

This is scientifically interesting.

It does **not** establish that consciousness persisted after irreversible brain death.

Cardiac arrest during active resuscitation and legally/medically established death by neurological criteria are not the same state.

A 2025 Nature Reviews Neurology article proposed a neuroscientific model for NDEs, showing that brain-based explanations remain an active and serious research program.

### Classification

**NDE PHENOMENOLOGY: ESTABLISHED OBSERVATION**

**MECHANISM: OPEN**

**PROOF OF AFTERLIFE: NOT ESTABLISHED**

---

# 20. Terminal and paradoxical lucidity

Episodes of unexpected lucidity have been reported in some people with severe dementia and other profound neurological impairment, including episodes near the end of life.

Modern reviews have found a real literature of:

- case reports;
- caregiver reports;
- qualitative studies;
- emerging prospective work.

The phenomenon is scientifically interesting precisely because its mechanism is poorly understood.

It may teach us about:

- residual network capacity;
- transient restoration of access;
- neuromodulation;
- memory retrieval;
- brain-state transitions;
- how severe structural disease relates to moment-to-moment function.

It does not by itself demonstrate that memory is stored outside the brain.

### Classification

**OBSERVED/REPORTED PHENOMENON: EVIDENCE EXISTS**

**MECHANISM: OPEN**

**NONLOCAL MEMORY / SOUL INTERPRETATION: SPECULATIVE**

---

# 21. Is there an afterlife?

This is one of humanity's oldest questions.

The project should separate three levels.

## Level 1 — empirical experiences around dying

Includes:

- NDEs;
- end-of-life dreams/visions;
- terminal/paradoxical lucidity.

These can be studied scientifically.

## Level 2 — anomalous-information claims

Includes controlled claims involving mediums or information apparently obtained without an identified conventional channel.

Some published meta-analytic and review literature reports above-chance effects in mediumship datasets.

These findings are **contested evidence** and do not by themselves establish survival after death.

Possible issues include:

- methodological heterogeneity;
- sensory/information leakage;
- statistical flexibility;
- publication and selection effects;
- replication;
- alternative psychological explanations;
- uncertainty over the physical mechanism.

## Level 3 — survival after irreversible death

The hypothesis that a personal conscious subject continues after irreversible death is a much stronger claim.

For physics, it would require evidence of:

- persistent information-bearing degrees of freedom;
- independence from the irreversibly nonfunctioning brain;
- a mechanism for identity/memory persistence;
- a reproducible channel by which that information affects measurements.

Phase 4 found **no established physical carrier or frequency** for such survival.

### Classification

**AFTERLIFE / POSTMORTEM PERSONAL SURVIVAL: OPEN PHILOSOPHICAL QUESTION + SPECULATIVE SCIENTIFIC HYPOTHESIS**

The atlas will not label it false.

It will not label it demonstrated.

# 22. Nonlocal or external memory hypotheses

A deeper version of the memory question proposes that the brain might access information stored outside ordinary neural tissue.

Possible versions include:

- memory as a nonlocal field;
- brain as receiver/filter rather than sole generator;
- quantum-nonlocal memory;
- survival-related memory access.

These ideas currently lack an established reproducible physical carrier and mechanism.

To enter the physical map as more than a question node, a model would need to specify:

```
information substrate
+ state variable
+ coupling to neurons
+ bandwidth / spectral behavior
+ energy/information accounting
+ shielding or distance dependence
+ falsifiable experiment
```

### Classification

**SPECULATIVE / NOT YET PHYSICALLY ESTABLISHED**

This is a useful placeholder because it states exactly what evidence would be needed.

# 22A. Telepathy, "mind reading," remote viewing and anomalous cognition

The phrase **mind reading** currently refers to at least two fundamentally different things.

## A. Technological neural decoding — established, but limited

Brain–computer-interface research can decode **measured neural activity** under constrained experimental conditions.

Examples include:

- decoding a small set of internally spoken words from implanted neural electrodes;
- decoding attempted or imagined speech for communication BCIs;
- classifying imagined speech from EEG with trained subject-specific models.

A 2024 Nature Human Behaviour study decoded internally spoken words from implanted recordings in two participants. Performance was above chance but varied substantially by participant and task.

This is genuine technological "mind reading" in a loose popular sense, but it requires:

```
physical access to brain signals
+ calibration/training
+ a constrained task vocabulary or statistical model
+ measurable neural activity
```

It is **not** unrestricted access to every thought, memory, belief or private mental image.

### Classification

**NEURAL DECODING: ESTABLISHED TECHNOLOGY / LIMITED DOMAIN**

---

## B. Telepathy — claimed person-to-person information transfer without known sensory channel

Telepathy proposes that information about one person's thoughts or target state can be acquired by another person without an established sensory, technological or inferential channel.

A major experimental tradition is the **Ganzfeld** paradigm.

The literature is contested.

A 2024 registered meta-analysis of more than four decades of Ganzfeld studies reported a small positive average effect.

However:

- earlier meta-analyses have produced both positive and null replication conclusions;
- methodological critiques have raised sensory-leakage, multiple-testing, publication, analytical and replication concerns;
- a 2026 critical review of neuroimaging ESP studies concluded that reported neural findings were heterogeneous and seldom replicated, and that definitive neural conclusions remain premature.

Therefore the project does not collapse "statistically interesting literature" into "telepathy established."

### Classification

**TELEPATHY: CONTESTED EVIDENCE / NO ESTABLISHED PHYSICAL CARRIER**

If telepathy were eventually established, the atlas would then ask:

```
What carries the information?
Does energy transfer occur?
Does signal strength depend on distance?
Can shielding alter it?
Is there a propagation speed?
Is there a spectrum or bandwidth?
Can the sender/receiver channel be independently detected?
Does it obey known information and thermodynamic constraints?
```

Until then, **frequency = unknown**.

---

## C. Remote viewing / clairvoyance

Remote-viewing and clairvoyance paradigms claim acquisition of information about a hidden or distant target without an identified conventional sensory channel.

Published studies include positive, null and controversial results.

A 2026 critical neuroimaging review retained both positive and null reports and concluded that the literature was too heterogeneous for a definitive neural signature.

### Classification

**ANOMALOUS COGNITION CLAIM: CONTESTED EVIDENCE / MECHANISM UNKNOWN**

Remote viewing should not automatically be merged with telepathy because a human "sender" is not required in every protocol.

---

## D. Precognition / presentiment

Precognition claims information about a future target or event before the ordinary causal information becomes available.

Presentiment experiments instead look for physiological changes before a later randomly selected stimulus.

These claims have a parapsychology literature but no established physical mechanism.

Because temporal ordering is central to the hypothesis, this category would require especially strict controls for:

- randomization;
- information leakage;
- optional stopping;
- analytical flexibility;
- expectation effects;
- multiple comparisons;
- preregistration;
- independent replication.

### Classification

**CONTESTED / SPECULATIVE MECHANISM**

---

## E. Why this belongs in the Universal Frequency Spectrum

If any anomalous-information effect were robustly demonstrated, one of the first physics questions would be whether it involves:

- an unknown field;
- a known field used in an unknown regime;
- a quantum-information effect;
- an emergent biological coupling;
- a non-wave/non-energy information mechanism;
- or a currently unidentified experimental artifact.

So the project should **reserve the space without filling in the answer**.

That is exactly what the unknown-carrier layer is for.

# PART III — TESLA, 3–6–9 & "ENERGY ALL AROUND US"

# 23. Did Tesla say 3, 6 and 9 were the key to the universe?

The widely circulated quotation:

> "If you only knew the magnificence of the 3, 6 and 9, then you would have the key to the universe."

is commonly attributed to Nikola Tesla.

However, the Tesla Universe archival quotation project states that it has **been unable to verify the quote**.

Phase 4 therefore does **not** use it as evidence of Tesla's scientific beliefs.

### Classification

**UNVERIFIED ATTRIBUTION / CULTURAL FOLKLORE**

There are genuine mathematical patterns involving 3, 6 and 9 — divisibility rules, modular arithmetic, digital roots, geometric symmetries — but those patterns do not establish a hidden physical law by themselves.

If the project later explores 3–6–9 mathematically, it should be a separate mathematical-pattern investigation with no assumption that Tesla originated the claim.

---

# 24. Tesla really did write about energy throughout space

Tesla did make an authentic, much more interesting statement in his 1891 lecture on high-frequency alternating currents.

In that lecture he argued that **"Throughout space there is energy"** and asked whether this energy was static or kinetic, speculating that humanity might someday couple machinery to natural energy flows.

The surrounding context matters.

Tesla discussed:

- high-frequency electricity;
- rarefied gases;
- radiant phenomena;
- heat;
- Crookes radiometers;
- wireless transmission;
- future energy technology.

This was visionary scientific speculation from 1891, not a demonstration of a previously unknown limitless energy field.

### Modern interpretation

Today we know space contains or carries many physically real forms of energy:

- electromagnetic radiation;
- thermal backgrounds;
- particle fields;
- gravitational fields;
- quantum vacuum structure;
- cosmic matter and dark-energy components.

Tesla's broad intuition that apparently empty space is not physically featureless aged surprisingly well.

But the modern theories are not evidence that Tesla had discovered dark energy, zero-point energy extraction, or a 3–6–9 law.

---

# 25. What would count as a genuinely new form of energy?

Physics usually treats energy as a conserved quantity/property rather than a substance.

So "new energy" would more precisely mean discovery of something such as:

- a new field;
- a new particle;
- a new interaction;
- a new phase of matter;
- a new energy-transfer mechanism;
- a new cosmological component;
- a violation/modification of known conservation assumptions under a new framework.

Examples of legitimate frontiers include:

- dark-sector fields;
- fifth-force mediators;
- dynamical dark energy;
- quantum-gravity degrees of freedom;
- unknown particles;
- novel collective excitations.

"Free energy" claims should be evaluated by measurable input/output energy accounting, not by terminology.

# 27. Phase 4 core conclusion

The project's frontier is now wider than dark matter, but not looser.

The correct structure is:

```
KNOWN PHENOMENON
      ↓
OPEN QUESTION
      ↓
HYPOTHESIS
      ↓
PHYSICAL MODEL
      ↓
PREDICTED OBSERVABLE
      ↓
EXPERIMENT
      ↓
CONSTRAINT / REPLICATION
      ↓
ESTABLISHED OR REJECTED
```

That pathway lets the atlas take consciousness, survival, Tesla, dark matter, quantum gravity, and unusual human experiences seriously **without giving every idea the same evidential weight**.

The purpose is not to protect current physics from strange ideas.

The purpose is to make strange ideas precise enough that nature gets a vote.
