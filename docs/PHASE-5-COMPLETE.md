## Scientific boundary

Phase 5 does not declare that the atlas has discovered a missing frequency, hidden force, telepathic carrier, unknown energy source, wormhole, soul field, or new fundamental physics.

It establishes the data structure required to recognize the difference between:

- evidence;
- prediction;
- unexplained observation;
- weak/contested result;
- untested hypothesis;
- missing instrumentation;
- missing theory;
- missing mechanism;
- and merely missing catalog content.
