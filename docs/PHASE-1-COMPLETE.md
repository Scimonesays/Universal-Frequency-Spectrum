## What Phase 1 establishes

The project can safely proceed from this statement:

> **Many physically distinct systems admit meaningful oscillatory or spectral descriptions. Frequency can serve as a common coordinate across them, while the underlying physical degree of freedom determines what is actually oscillating.**

## What Phase 1 does not establish

Phase 1 does not claim:

- that all matter has one characteristic frequency;
- that all waves share one medium;
- that sound transforms continuously into light merely by increasing frequency;
- that heat is an electromagnetic frequency band;
- that a blank region of a chart predicts unknown physics;
- that dark matter has a known frequency;
- that gravity has one characteristic frequency;
- that quantum field theory proves a single universal vibration.

## Known limitations intentionally deferred

Phase 1 does **not** attempt to provide exhaustive numeric frequency ranges. That work belongs to Phase 2, where every range will be checked for what the numbers actually represent: physical mode, conventional band, biological sensitivity, theoretical prediction, observed range, or detector sensitivity.

Phase 1 also does not yet settle difficult classification cases such as neutrino flavor oscillations, cosmological expansion timescales, quasi-periodic astrophysical processes, turbulence spectra, or every condensed-matter quasiparticle. These will be admitted only after the ontology can describe them without forcing unlike meanings of "frequency" into the same box.
