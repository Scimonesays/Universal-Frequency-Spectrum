## Key result

Phase 2 falsifies the idea that a scientifically honest atlas can use only:

`name + min_frequency + max_frequency`

The observed physics requires several representational types:

- continuous band;
- characteristic line;
- dispersion relation;
- parameterized frequency;
- representative observational range;
- detector band;
- biological/conventional band;
- state-dependent quantum behavior.

## Scientific boundary carried forward

The project may compare different phenomena on a common frequency axis **only when the meaning of that frequency remains attached to the entry**.

A shared x-coordinate is not evidence of a shared physical mechanism.

## Deferred to Phase 3

Phase 2 catalogs the nodes.

Phase 3 will research and encode the **edges**:

- drives;
- excites;
- absorbs;
- emits;
- scatters;
- converts;
- couples;
- detects;
- transduces.

That is where the atlas begins to explain how energy moves between these otherwise separate rows.
