# Universal Frequency Spectrum

> **Working subtitle:** The Periodic Map of Waves, Oscillations & Fields

A research repository for building a scientifically sourced map of physical oscillations across frequency space.

The project begins with a simple question:

**If two things have a frequency, what is actually oscillating in each case?**

Sound, electromagnetic radiation, molecular vibration, lattice vibration, matter waves, gravitational waves, and hypothetical oscillating dark-sector fields can all be described using frequency. That common coordinate is useful — but it does **not** mean they are the same physical phenomenon.

This repository is intended to make those relationships visible without blurring established physics into speculation.

## Scientific ground rules

1. **Frequency is a measurement, not an ontology.** Sharing a frequency does not establish a shared physical substrate.
2. **Always identify what is oscillating.** Matter, pressure/density, a field, a normal mode, a quantum state, or spacetime geometry are not interchangeable.
3. **Separate propagation medium from physical degree of freedom.**
4. **Do not treat heat as a single frequency band.** Thermal radiation, conduction, convection, lattice excitations, and other thermal processes must be distinguished.
5. **Do not assume a "universal vibration."** If a unifying hypothesis is explored later, it must be labeled as a hypothesis rather than built into the map.
6. **Do not infer new physics from an empty-looking frequency interval alone.** A meaningful gap must be defined relative to a physical family, theory, observable, and detector/measurement coverage.
7. **Established, model-dependent, open, and speculative claims must remain visibly distinct.**
8. **Every scientific claim intended for the final atlas must be source-traceable.**
9. **Every important energy claim must name the system boundary.** "Energy decreases," "energy is used," or "energy is produced" is incomplete until the source, destination, conversion, and losses are identified.
10. **Do not label ordinary processes as creating energy from nothing.** Use storage, release, transfer, conversion, work, radiation, dissipation, thermalization, dispersion, or steady-state balance unless new physics is actually demonstrated.

## Proposed organizing dimensions

The final representation should not be a single "low frequency → high frequency" line. A more defensible structure is multidimensional:

- **Frequency / period** — common horizontal coordinate, normally logarithmic.
- **Frequency semantics** — whether the number is an oscillation, wave, resonance, transition, detector band, rate, inverse timescale, classification band, or energy-equivalent frequency.
- **What oscillates?** — the primary physical family.
- **Medium or substrate** — if one is required.
- **Propagation speed / dispersion relation** — where meaningful.
- **Wavelength / wavevector** — where meaningful.
- **Energy relation** — only where the governing physics justifies it.
- **Energy accounting** — system boundary, storage, gain/loss, transfer, conversion, work, emission, dissipation, dispersion, steady-state balance, and unknowns.
- **Scale** — macroscopic, molecular, atomic, nuclear, field, cosmological.
- **Coupling / conversion pathways** — how energy or information moves between families.
- **Evidence status** — established, model-dependent, open, speculative.
- **Detection coverage** — what instruments actually probe.

## The intended result

The long-term goal is a visual and machine-readable atlas that lets a person pick a frequency and ask:

- What known phenomena can occur here?
- What exactly is oscillating?
- Does it need matter to propagate?
- What theory describes it?
- What other modes can it couple to?
- How is it detected?
- What happens to energy across the stated system boundary?
- Which regions are measured, predicted, weakly constrained, or genuinely unknown?

The atlas should be useful precisely because **overlap is allowed**. A 100 Hz acoustic wave, a 100 Hz electromagnetic signal, and a 100 Hz gravitational wave can share a number while belonging to fundamentally different rows.

## Citation philosophy

Documents include **Quick sources** sections linking directly to scientific papers, reviews, standards bodies, or authoritative scientific institutions. The project favors primary literature and high-quality reviews; educational sources may clarify definitions but should not carry controversial or frontier claims by themselves.
