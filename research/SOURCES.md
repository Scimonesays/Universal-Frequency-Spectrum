## Source-use rule

A paper's presence in this library does not automatically validate every possible claim derived from it.

When adding a dataset record:

1. cite the exact claim the source supports;
2. state whether the source is measurement, theory, review, or institutional reference;
3. preserve model assumptions;
4. preserve the meaning of the quoted frequency range;
5. do not turn a detector window into a universal physical boundary.
